His rules, and how the app uses them

1. Only the leaders. Six momentum scans: biggest gainers over 1, 3 and 6 months, measured from the close 21/63/126 days ago and from the lowest low in that period. Only the top ~7% of each (max 50) are kept, after filtering for ADR ≥ 5% and at least $5M traded a day.

2. The setup. Rising 20 and 50-day averages; the stock is surfing its 10, 20 or 50-day simple moving average; higher lows; range not widening; near its highs. Extra stars for a tight last 3 days, a narrow candle the day before, a steep trend, relative strength vs the Nasdaq, a hot sector, volume drying up, and appearing in several scans.

3. Entry. Buy as it breaks the trigger (top of the range), at the opening range high (first 1, 5 or 60-minute candle). Don't buy if it's already above the too late price (yesterday's close + one ATR). Never within ~3 days of earnings.

4. Stop. Low of the day you buy. Must not be wider than the ATR; his are usually about half an ATR.

5. Selling. Ignore days 1–2. After 3–5 days sell 1/3 to 1/2 and move the stop to break-even. Sell the rest on the first close below the 10-day average.

6. Market. Only trade breakouts when the Nasdaq light is green (10 and 20-day averages rising, 10 above 20). In red markets most breakouts fail; sit in cash.
